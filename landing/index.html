FAQ

Questions, answered

What is AdVault Spy?

AdVault Spy is a Chrome extension that scans the webpage you're viewing for ads, analyzes their creative strategy with AI, generates marketing hooks, and identifies promising business prospects from public page data.

How does scanning work?

Open any webpage, click the AdVault Spy icon, and run a scan. The extension detects ad candidates and advertising signals on the page you're already viewing, then builds an intelligence report you can explore, analyze and export.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan includes 2 scans per day with page scanning, AI ad analysis, marketing hook generation and prospect discovery.

What are the Pro and Agency plans?

Pro ($19/month) and Agency ($49/month) are planned as paid tiers with unlimited scans, advanced AI analysis and higher limits. Billing is not live yet — no checkout is available today.

How does the Chrome extension work?

Install the extension, open any public webpage, and run a scan. Everything is built around the page you're currently viewing — you can sign in from the landing page and scan right away.

How does AI analysis work?

Detected ad text and details are sent to the product's analysis service, which returns viral hooks, improved copy, marketing angles and an explanation of why the ad works. If the service is unavailable, the extension shows a local fallback.

Does AdVault Spy scrape private information?

No. AdVault works only with what is already publicly visible on the page you choose to inspect, and scans run inside your browser. Review the extension's data handling before using it on sensitive pages.

Can I use it for competitor research?

Yes. Analyzing publicly displayed ads on a competitor's page is one of the core use cases for the tool.

When will accounts and billing be available?

Accounts are live now — sign up free from any "Start Free" button on this page. Pro and Agency billing are still in development.
